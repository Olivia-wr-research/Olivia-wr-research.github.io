# Before / After Website Summary

## Before

- Homepage mixed academic identity, firm innovation, personal hobbies, and working-style descriptors.
- Research page was organised around two broad streams rather than supervisor-facing questions.
- Publications page used output labels but did not make the full status hierarchy explicit.
- CV page only exposed a public CV download and contact block.
- No For Collaboration page or one-page research collaboration profile was available.

## After

- Homepage now presents one coherent identity: scientific mobility, knowledge networks, and innovation.
- Research page is organised by three research questions and evidence-backed research pillars.
- Publications page separates accepted, submitted, master's thesis, selected research output, and research software/data products.
- Methods & Data page distinguishes data systems, measurement, empirical design, and reproducible software workflows.
- For Collaboration page states research objects, data capabilities, analytical capabilities, and current 2027 interests.
- CV page links both the global no-photo CV and the separate Research & Collaboration Profile PDF.
