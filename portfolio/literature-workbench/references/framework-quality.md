# Theoretical Framework Quality Criteria

A strong framework should answer:

1. **What is being explained?** Clear focal outcome/explanandum.
2. **At what level?** Individual, dyad, organization, city, institution, country, system, multilevel.
3. **What are the constructs?** Non-overlapping definitions.
4. **Why should X affect Y?** Explicit mechanism, not an arrow without logic.
5. **When/for whom?** Boundary conditions and moderators.
6. **What alternative explanation competes?** Rival mechanism/theory.
7. **What would falsify the framework?** Discriminating prediction or observable implication.
8. **What is inherited vs novel?** Literature-supported components separated from new synthesis.
9. **Is it parsimonious?** Remove duplicate constructs and decorative arrows.
10. **Can it connect to empirical design?** Constructs and relationships are operationalizable in principle.

## Common failure modes

- taxonomy presented as theory;
- policy categories connected directly to outcomes without mechanism;
- many arrows but no temporal ordering;
- identical constructs under different labels;
- level-of-analysis mismatch;
- framework simply restates dependent variables;
- every possible moderator added without theoretical reason;
- novelty claimed because two literatures use different vocabulary;
- propositions that cannot distinguish the framework from rivals.
