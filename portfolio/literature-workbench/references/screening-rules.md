# Screening Rules

## Two-stage default

### Stage 1 — Title/abstract
Classify: INCLUDE / EXCLUDE / UNCERTAIN.

### Stage 2 — Full text
Resolve UNCERTAIN and confirm theoretical/empirical relevance.

## Every exclusion needs a reason

Use controlled reason codes when possible:

- OUTCOME_MISMATCH
- POPULATION_MISMATCH
- INTERVENTION_OR_CONTEXT_MISMATCH
- WRONG_DOCUMENT_TYPE
- NOT_EMPIRICAL_WHEN_EMPIRICAL_REQUIRED
- NOT_THEORETICAL_WHEN_THEORY_REQUIRED
- LANGUAGE_OUT_OF_SCOPE
- DATE_OUT_OF_SCOPE
- DUPLICATE
- RETRACTED_OR_INVALID
- INSUFFICIENT_ACCESS_FOR_REQUIRED_EXTRACTION
- OTHER_WITH_NOTE

## Borderline handling

Do not force ambiguous cases into EXCLUDE for convenience. Maintain `UNCERTAIN` until the missing criterion can be checked.

## Screening is not ranking

A lower-ranked venue may still be essential if it is foundational, uniquely relevant, or provides contradictory evidence. Keep relevance and source quality as separate fields.
