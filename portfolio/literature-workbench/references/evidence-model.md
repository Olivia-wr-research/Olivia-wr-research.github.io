# Evidence Model

## Five epistemic types

### SOURCE_FACT
Directly verifiable information: bibliographic metadata, sample sizes, variables, methods, coefficients, quoted text, dates, tables and figures.

### AUTHOR_CLAIM
An interpretation, mechanism, theory statement, limitation or implication explicitly advanced by the paper authors.

### AGENT_INTERPRETATION
An analyst's inference from the source. It must never be silently attributed to the source authors.

### CROSS_SOURCE_SYNTHESIS
A pattern that emerges from two or more source records. It should identify supporting and contradictory sources.

### PROPOSED_FRAMEWORK
A new integrative model, taxonomy, hypothesis, proposition, mechanism extension, or research agenda. It belongs to the present analysis, not the prior literature.

## Claim ledger rule

Every material sentence intended for final academic prose should be classifiable as one of these five types and traceable to a `claim_id` or explicit framework proposition.

## Contradictions

Contradictory/null findings are first-class evidence. Never overwrite them with a majority-vote summary. Record:

- exact point of disagreement;
- differences in design/context/measurement;
- whether the contradiction is real or only apparent;
- whether it implies a boundary condition.
