# Methods Mapping

## Workflow

1. Read the evidence matrix and original sources as needed.
2. Populate `07_methods/methods_matrix.csv`.
3. Classify design family and inferential strength.
4. Compare sample construction, unit of analysis, geography, period, and data sources.
5. Track construct operationalization and measurement validity problems.
6. Record the actual identification strategy rather than the method label alone.
7. Determine whether causal language is supported.
8. Identify robustness/validation practices.
9. Distinguish a genuine method gap from “this method has not been used yet.”
10. Link method differences to contradictory substantive findings when plausible.

## Output

Return method clusters, recurring weaknesses, comparability limits, and evidence-backed opportunities for stronger design.
