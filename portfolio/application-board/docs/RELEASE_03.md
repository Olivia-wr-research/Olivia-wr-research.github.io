## Freshness and release policy

This is a manually dated snapshot. No unattended refresh is configured. Before adding a record, inspect the original employer page, capture source, deadline, requirements and unknowns, omit contacts, and test its structure. The browser hides past-date snapshots by default; that is not proof of current recruitment status. Imported snapshots remain explicitly unverified and never inherit official verification claims. No third-party radar dataset is mirrored.

## Pilot boundaries

No real users have been recruited or measured. No conversion, adoption, accessibility certification, semantic-match accuracy or comprehensive European coverage is claimed. Browser preferences are unencrypted and device-local; backup remains available through the tracker. The next user study should observe finding a relevant position, explaining why, saving it and returning, with explicit consent and without collecting CVs or unpublished research.
