# 0.6 — Discovery before promotion

Released catalog: 75 employer advertisements across five countries, compiled 2026-10-16. Counts describe advertisements, not individual funded places. Coverage remains concentrated in Switzerland and Norway; this is not a comprehensive or continuously live European database. An unknown deadline is not evidence that applications remain open.

## Implemented

- Chinese and English discovery interface, titles, summaries and requirements; warm burgundy/gold app layout and 20-card progressive loading.
- Guided exploration and browser-local CV parsing retained. Users confirm suggested interests. No CV upload, model inference or automatic application.
- Matching sorts shared topics and methods instead of presenting a fit percentage. Country aliases and bilingual method aliases are normalized.
- ETH public-listing importer and University of Oslo Jobbnorge feed importer produce review queues, never automatic publication. Singapore-based ETH listings are excluded from this European release.
- Canonical URL deduplication, schema validation and change reports. Missing listings require review rather than automatically being called closed.
- Application saves re-read current storage; tracker rejects stale writes from another tab. Browser-local storage is not cloud backup.

## Before broad invitations

Add more independently maintained institutional feeds and monitor freshness. Review translated requirements against full employer pages; short summaries are not complete eligibility assessments. Test complete search-to-save journeys with real users before claiming validated demand. No completed interviews, revenue or semantic-matching benchmark are claimed.

## Commercial hypothesis

Keep discovery, bilingual reading and basic saving free. Test recurring value around maintained collections, useful change alerts and application planning before any candidate subscription. Employer annual recruitment plans require demonstrable audience reach and transparent sponsored placement, separate from relevance ranking. No payment feature is implemented.
