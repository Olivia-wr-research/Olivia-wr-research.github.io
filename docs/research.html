Research Focus

My research examines the spatial organization of talent and firm innovation activity. At this stage, it is organized around two research streams with a shared concern for regional innovation.

Talent Mobility, Collaboration Networks, and Regional Innovation

This stream studies the career mobility of highly skilled workers and scientists, with particular attention to how people move across cities and institutions. It asks how urban opportunity structures, prior collaboration ties, multidimensional proximity, and research platforms are associated with mobility networks, career trajectories, and destination choice. The scale of analysis moves from city-level mobility networks to individual career histories and event-level comparison among candidate destinations.

Firm Innovation and Innovation Measurement

This stream studies leading global R&D investors through a firm-year data system that links R&D expenditure, patents, trademarks, industry, country, firm identity, and year. It focuses on the measurement, combination, and temporal change of technological and brand-oriented innovation, with attention to typological differences, path dependence, and firm heterogeneity.

Talent Mobility, Collaboration Networks, and Regional Innovation

Intercity Mobility Networks of Elite Biomedical Scientists

This work reconstructs the career histories of highly cited biomedical scientists and organizes intercity employment moves as directed ties among cities. The analysis treats cities as institutional and scientific opportunity environments, then examines the spatial structure and city-level conditions associated with mobility-link formation.

Data Longitudinal career histories · institution-city matching · mobility events

Methods Social network analysis · city-level indicators · GIS interpretation · Heckman

Accepted article in World Regional Studies.

Whole-Network and Ego-Network Extension

The next stage connects city-level network structure with scientist-level mobility ego-networks. A 1970-2023 longitudinal database of 500 elite biomedical scientists and a 40-scientist ego-network subset support analysis of how individual careers are embedded in broader urban mobility networks.

Data Scientist-institution-city histories · mobility ego-network subset

Methods Whole-network analysis · ego networks · proximity measures · WERGM

Submitted manuscript.

Destination Choice and Relational Mechanisms

Recent work treats an observed intercity job move as a choice among candidate destinations. It compares the selected city with alternative cities and measures pre-move coauthor ties before each move, including tie presence, breadth, persistence, network share, timing, and domestic or international context.

Data Mobility event by candidate-destination structure · time-ordered coauthor ties

Methods Candidate sets · event-stratified conditional logit · temporal design

Submitted manuscript.

Firm Innovation and Innovation Measurement

Firm-Year Innovation Data System

This work develops a firm-year data system for leading global R&D investors. It links R&D expenditure with patents, trademarks, industry, country, firm identity, and year so that technological and market-facing innovation can be studied in the same empirical structure.

Data R&D investors · patents · trademarks · industry and country fields

Methods Entity matching · IP linkage · panel construction · measurement audit

Data-system development in progress.

Technology-Brand Innovation Combinations

This line asks how patent portfolios and trademark portfolios combine across firms. The emphasis is on measurement: how to compare technology-oriented and brand-oriented innovation without collapsing firm innovation into a single output count.

Data Linked intellectual-property records · firm-year observations

Methods Indicator construction · portfolio comparison · typological description

Research design and data construction in progress.

Dynamic Evolution and Spatial Context

The firm-year panel also supports questions about change over time. Current design work considers how innovation configurations vary by industry, country, firm characteristics, and regional innovation environments.

Data Firm-year innovation records · industry and national context

Methods Longitudinal comparison · heterogeneity analysis · spatial contextualization

Ongoing research stream.

Research Trajectory

01

Spatial and Quantitative Foundations

Remote sensing, trade networks, and applied quantitative training

Early projects moved from spatial data processing, urban resilience, and global trade networks into quantitative research training, building a foundation for analyzing spatial data, temporal change, and relational networks.

GIS · VIIRS-DNB · DTW · K-Means · UN Comtrade · Network Analysis

02

Talent Mobility Networks

From career histories to city-level mobility networks

By reconstructing scientist career histories and matching institutions with cities, individual career moves are organized as directed mobility relations among cities. This supports analysis of mobility structure, city hierarchy, and network formation conditions.

Career Histories · SNA · GIS · Heckman

03

Multi-scale Relational Analysis

From whole networks to individual career trajectories

The research then moves from the overall city network to individual career trajectories, mobility ego-networks, and multidimensional proximity, connecting macro mobility structure with micro relational organization.

WERGM · Ego Networks · Proximity · Longitudinal Data

04

Event-level Choice Design

From realized moves to candidate-destination comparison

Each observed career move is modelled as an event-level choice among candidate cities, making it possible to compare selected and unselected destinations through pre-move collaboration ties and their scale, persistence, and timing.

Candidate Sets · Conditional Logit · Coauthor Ties · Temporal Design

05

Innovation Measurement Extension

From talent as an innovation actor to firms

The current extension turns to leading global R&D investors and builds innovation measures from R&D expenditure, patents, trademarks, and firm-year information.

R&D · Patents · Trademarks · Firm-Year Panel
